games that run in a browser. Nothing to install, no accounts to make, and once a page has loaded it keeps working with no signal.

On a phone? Open a game, then add it to your home screen — iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: the browser menu, then Add to Home screen. It opens full screen after that, like an app.

Both games are free, have no ads and take no payment of any kind. Nothing you do in them leaves your device except Side Pot’s connection to the people you are playing with.
